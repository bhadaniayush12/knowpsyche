Fair questions

Is it really free?+

Yes. Every test and every result is free, with no sign-up. The site is supported by ads.

How accurate are these tests?+

They're based on well-known psychology experiments and questionnaires. Great for learning about yourself — but one result is a snapshot, not a final answer.

Do you save my answers?+

No. Your answers are scored on your own device and never sent to us.

Is this a medical or mental-health test?+

No. KnowPsyche is for learning and self-reflection. It can't diagnose anything — for that, please talk to a professional.
