01

What we collect

Your test answers and scores are calculated on your own device and are not sent to our servers. If you share a result, the share image is created in your browser too.

Like most websites, our host may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure and working. We measure visits with [ANALYTICS TOOL], which reports totals, not individual people.

02

Cookies & advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, through a Google-certified consent tool.

03

Your choices

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the tests will still work.

04

KnowPsyche is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
