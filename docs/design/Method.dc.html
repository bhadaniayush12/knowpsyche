Our method · Fig. 00.2 · Evidence before certainty

How our tests work —
and where they stop.

Short version: honest tasks, transparent scoring, every limit written down. Here is the long version.

01 · Where questions come from

Cognitive tasks are rebuilt from classic experiments described in research. Personality items are written by us or adapted from public-domain pools such as the International Personality Item Pool (IPIP). We never copy commercial tests or use their trademarked names.

02 · How scoring works

Scoring runs in your browser, and each test page shows its formula. Reaction time, for example, is the average of five timed tries. Nothing you answer is sent to us.

03 · What a result means

A result describes how you did on one task, on one day, on one device. We show ranges, not verdicts — and never claim to measure clinical conditions or certify IQ.

  • Sell “certified” IQ scores
  • Lock results behind an email or payment
  • Store your answers on our servers
