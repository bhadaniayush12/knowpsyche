A result is a clue, not a conclusion.
The problem

Most test sites hide your result behind an email form, sell a “certified” score, or hand you a label that sounds like a diagnosis. You leave knowing a word about yourself — but not what it means, or how much to trust it.

Our answer

KnowPsyche puts the test first, shows every score, and explains the psychology behind it — what the task measures, what it can't, and where to read more. And it teaches the psychology behind every result, in plain words.

Six promises

What you can count on.

01

Free, no email gate

Every result is shown in full, straight away. No sign-up, no paywall.

02

Your answers stay with you

Tests are scored inside your browser. We don't store your answers or scores.

03

Explain, don't label

Each result comes with what it means, what it doesn't, and how context changes it.

04

Original questions

Written by us or adapted from public-domain sources — never copied from paid tests.

05

Never a diagnosis

We don't test for clinical conditions or hand out “certified IQ” scores.

06

Cite and correct

Sources are listed on each page. Spot a mistake? We fix it and say so.

Who is behind this

An independent project, built out of curiosity.

Hi, I'm Dev. I'm a commerce student in Kolkata, specialising in marketing, and I build small, free web tools on the side.

Studying marketing showed me how easily the mind can be nudged. That made me curious about how it really works, and it's why KnowPsyche uses psychology only to make things clearer, never to push you. I built it because I wanted a place where trying a test is the start of understanding, not the end of it.

I'm not a psychologist. Every test is built from published research, and each page lists its sources so you can check the work. KnowPsyche is not a clinic and has no connection to any test publisher.

How the site pays for itself

KnowPsyche is free because it shows ads from Google AdSense around the content — never inside a test while you're taking it. We don't sell personal data, and your test answers never leave your browser.
