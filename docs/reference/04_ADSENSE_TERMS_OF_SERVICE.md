# Google AdSense Online Terms of Service

## Scope

This file organizes the supplied Google AdSense Online Terms of Service into audit-relevant sections while preserving the substantive requirements from the source material.

> These Terms are contractual terms. They should not be treated as a simple website checklist.

# 1. Welcome to AdSense

Using the Services means agreeing to:

1. The AdSense Terms of Service
2. AdSense Program Policies, including the policy families referenced by Google
3. Google Branding Guidelines

The supplied source states that, in the event of conflict, the Terms of Service take precedence over the other enumerated policies/guidelines.

Use of AdSense is subject to:

- Creation of an AdSense Account
- Google's approval of that account

Google may refuse or limit access.

The supplied source states that, for an individual applicant:

- The applicant represents that they are at least 18 years old.
- Only one AdSense Account may be held.
- Creating multiple accounts can lead to loss of payment entitlement and account termination.

Google may request additional information to verify an account, including name, address, and other identifying information.

Google may access, index, and cache approved properties, including through automated means, and may refuse services to a property.

Google may modify the AdSense Terms. The supplied Terms say changes generally become effective 14 days after posting, except changes for new functions or legal reasons, which may take effect immediately.

# 5. Payment and invalid activity

Payments depend on continued compliance with the AdSense Terms.

Google can withhold or adjust payments for invalid activity.

The supplied source identifies examples of invalid activity including:

- Spam
- Invalid clicks
- Invalid impressions
- Invalid queries
- Invalid conversions
- Automated/bot-generated activity
- Activity originating from computers/IPs under the publisher's control
- Solicited clicks or impressions
- Requests for users to click ads or take related actions
- Activity involving properties that do not comply with policy
- Other significant or intentional invalid activity

Google may debit an account and adjust future payments for invalid activity.

# 6. Termination and suspension

Google may suspend payments, suspend or terminate a property's participation, or suspend/terminate an account for reasons including:

- Invalid activity

The supplied Terms state that an account closed for inactivity after six or more consecutive months may be treated differently from termination for policy breach.

If Google terminates an account for breach, the supplied Terms describe restrictions on creating a new account and possible restrictions on monetizing content on other Google products.

## Website-audit items extracted from the Terms

For a normal website applying for AdSense, an AI agent should verify:

### Ownership/control
- The publisher owns or is legally authorized to act for the site.
- The publisher controls the technical/editorial implementation.

### HTML access
- The site can accept AdSense code in the required location.
- The owner can modify the site's HTML/source through the relevant stack/CMS.

### Privacy
- A clearly labeled, easily accessible privacy policy exists.
- It addresses cookies and relevant device/data collection and usage.

### Accuracy
- Publisher-provided information is accurate and current.
- Site-related advertising declarations/metadata are not misleading.

### Policy compliance
- The property complies with the applicable Program Policies.

### Account discipline
- Do not create multiple AdSense accounts in conflict with the Terms.

### Invalid activity
- Never encourage/cause invalid clicks or impressions.
- Avoid artificial traffic or automated activity intended to inflate advertising metrics.
